About Me

Professional Summary

I am an HSE professional with over 17 years of experience, currently working as an HSE Section Head, leading safety systems and teams across complex industrial environments. I have strong experience in implementing HSE management systems aligned with international standards, improving safety performance, and driving a strong safety culture across sites. Recently, I have been focusing on integrating digital tools and AI technologies into HSE processes, including data analytics for KPI tracking, automated reporting, and improving decision-making through insights from safety data. I believe that the future of HSE is not only compliance, but also digital transformation, predictive risk management, and smarter safety systems. My goal is to continue growing as an HSE leader who combines field experience with digital innovation to build safer and more efficient workplaces. I'm effectively looking for a new challenge in my HSE career, I'm very hard working individual, working under pressure and eager to learn and broaden my horizons.
